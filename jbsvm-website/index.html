About Our College

Garavpur, Lambhua, Sultanpur

Jeet Bahadur Singh Vidhi Mahavidyalay

Jeet Bahadur Singh Vidhi Mahavidyalay, situated at Garavpur, Lambhua in District Sultanpur (Uttar Pradesh), is dedicated to providing quality legal education to students of the region. The college offers the LL.B 3 Year Course and is affiliated to Dr. Ram Manohar Lohiya Avadh University, Ayodhya (U.P.) and recognized by the Bar Council of India, New Delhi.

Our aim is to prepare students for careers at the Bar, in the judiciary, in public service and in the corporate world, with a strong foundation in law, ethics and social justice.

  • Moot court & practical legal training
  • Well-stocked law library

Course Overview

The LL.B 3 Year Course is a professional degree for graduates who wish to build a career in law. After completing the course, students can enrol as advocates with the State Bar Council and appear for judicial services and other competitive examinations.

Eligibility

Graduation in any discipline from a recognized university, with minimum marks as prescribed by the Bar Council of India and Dr. Ram Manohar Lohiya Avadh University, Ayodhya.
